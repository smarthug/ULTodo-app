ULTodo Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

ULTodo is a local-first productivity app for capturing tasks, prioritizing work, planning today's focus, and using a Pomodoro timer.

Data collection

ULTodo does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, cloud sync, crash reporting, or third-party tracking SDKs in the current release.

Local task data

Tasks, projects, tags, settings, and Pomodoro state are stored locally on your device using the app's WebView storage. This data is used only to provide the app's task management features and is not sent to the developer or to a server.

Internet permission

The Android app includes the standard Internet permission because it is packaged with Capacitor WebView infrastructure. The current release does not use this permission to upload your tasks or collect personal data.

Data deletion

You can delete task data inside the app where deletion controls are available. Removing the app from your device also removes app-local storage managed by the operating system.

Changes

If future releases add sync, accounts, analytics, crash reporting, notifications, or any other data collection, this policy and the Google Play Data safety information will be updated before release.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact email shown on ULTodo's Google Play listing.
